## 🔥 The Fun Stuff I've Built

### 🧩 **Jigsaw Puzzle Attack** - *When ML Models Meet Their Match*
Remember when everyone said "ML will solve cybersecurity"? Well, I spent my time proving they were a bit... optimistic. I reproduced a semantic backdoor attack that basically makes malware detectors say "this is fine 🔥" while everything burns. Used binary rewriting, assembly manipulation, and enough reverse engineering to make my CPU cry.

**Stack:** Python, PyTorch, Capstone, Keystone, Unicorn, and a lot of coffee ☕

### 🎯 **DistributedQ** - *Because Murphy's Law is Real*
Built a distributed task processing system that refuses to die. Seriously. It has more recovery mechanisms than a Hollywood action hero. When your task fails, it retries. When a node crashes, it redistributes. When everything goes wrong, it... still works.

**Secret sauce:** Go, Redis, Prometheus, Grafana, and an unhealthy obsession with fault tolerance.

### 🔐 **StickerSense** - *Privacy > Features*
Created a system that processes your data without, you know, actually seeing your data. Everything happens locally on your device because I believe your business is YOUR business, not mine, not some cloud server's, and definitely not some marketing database's.

**Philosophy:** Privacy-by-Design isn't just a buzzword, it's the default setting.

### 🏫 **Club Inglese Platform** - *My Digital Transformation Baby*
Turned a paper-based educational institute into a digital powerhouse. Built the entire platform from scratch: Spring Boot backend, secure authentication, role-based access control, and a UI that even the least tech-savvy teachers can use (that was the real challenge, trust me).

**Achievement unlocked:** Zero security breaches, countless paper cuts avoided, and one very happy administration team.
